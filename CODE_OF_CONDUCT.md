# Code of conduct

## Our standard

We want this project to be a welcoming place to learn, contribute, and share creative work. Be respectful, give actionable feedback, assume good intent, and make room for people with different experience levels and backgrounds.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, publishing another person’s private information, and sustained disruption of project discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not follow this standard. Repeated or serious violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact options on the [GitHub profile](https://github.com/t1seo). Reports will be reviewed fairly and kept confidential as far as practical.

This standard applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing the project.
