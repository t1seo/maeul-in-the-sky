A year in your village

Four seasons. A changing world.

Seasons follow each date. Choose your hemisphere to bring spring, summer, autumn, and winter home.

  • Spring

    Blossoms, fresh shoots, and the first soft greens.

  • Summer

    Full leafy canopies, lush fields, and life by the water.

  • Autumn

    Amber leaves, golden harvests, and warm earth.

  • Winter

    Snow-capped roofs, frosted trees, and quiet branches.

Southern hemisphere seasons shift by half a year. Your contribution dates stay the same.

Little by little

Every day leaves its own mark.

Daily contributions grow more than the height of your terrain. Reach a new range to give that day a richer main feature.

GitHub contributions per day

  1. 0A quiet dayResting terrain
  2. 1–4First signsA little life appears
  3. 5–9Taking rootA fuller feature
  4. 10–24Growing steadilyA more developed scene
  5. 25–49A thriving dayA substantial centerpiece
  6. 50+A standout dayThe richest daily feature

At 25 and 50 contributions, the main feature becomes more substantial, even when terrain height has reached its limit. Density and other busy days do not lower a day's growth. Wonders are extra discoveries alongside this daily growth.

Groves, meadows, and waterside gardens lead the landscape. Buildings and boats appear as occasional landmarks among varied natural shapes.

Keep returning to see more life: 5, 12, and 20 active days in the past 28 calendar days enrich seasonal petals, fireflies, leaves, or frost. Rest days are welcome. Only supplied activity counts, and the details remain visible with motion turned off.

These are playful visual milestones, not a measure of code quality. Open Explore a day to see its contributions and discoveries.
